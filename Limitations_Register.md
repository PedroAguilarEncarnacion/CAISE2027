## Discrepancy Classification
- **Implementation:** the program omits a relationship or explanation required by the supported specification.
- **Specification:** the procedure cannot justify a step without an additional rule or assumption.
- **Insufficient foundation:** a case judgment lacks sufficient evidence, scope, or justification.
- **Evaluation design:** expectations or cases cannot distinguish competing explanations.

Do not automatically attribute a discrepancy to the conceptual model. First locate the step and compare it with the specification and case record. L11 is an explanatory limitation of the implemented output, not a refutation of SC1.1.

## Preservation Rule
Evaluated artifacts are not altered to remove discrepancies. A later correction requires a new version, change record, and separate execution. Reports preserve the original results. English narrative translations are documented as a language edition and do not retrospectively correct the R-02 discrepancy.
