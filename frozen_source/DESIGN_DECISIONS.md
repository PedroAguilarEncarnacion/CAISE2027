## Risks to Examine
A warrant object does not establish that its contents are scientifically grounded. A declared alternative set does not establish exhaustive coverage. A recorded review does not demonstrate identification of every objection. The implementation makes these commitments inspectable; it does not eliminate them.

Human methods are included in the snapshot. Computational checks implement a particular interpretation of SC1.1. If that interpretation permits an unjustified step or improperly blocks another, record a possible specification or operationalization limitation rather than automatically attributing it to the conceptual model.

## Pending Human Review
An independent evaluator must reconstruct the conditions and conclusion from the output, snapshot, and declared procedure. Record unreconstructable steps, missing judgments, and discrepancies. No reviewer names, agreements, or outcomes have been fabricated.
