# Claim Analysis Report — v1 — English translation

## Research Question
How do evidence supporting an anticipated user need, interaction conditions, intervention properties, and expected consequences relate, and how can these relationships be interpreted and analysed to support an explicit assessment of proactive intervention justification?

The model proposes these relationships. This evaluation examines a qualitative operationalization using curated judgments. The results provide evidence of the procedure's applicability and limitations. They do not establish that a real intervention is justified or that the input judgments are adequate.

## CL1 — Operationalizability
A-01 ends undetermined and B-00 favourably supported under stipulated synthetic premises. The procedure handles missing domain information without confusing it with broken references. The evidence demonstrates an executable interpretation of the relationships, not automation of every scientific inference. Relevance, warrant support, criticality, and response acceptance are researcher-supplied judgments.

Completion of thirteen cases extends the technical examination; it does not constitute thirteen independent corroborations. The variants derive from one synthetic base. Results are conditional on the implemented patterns.

## CL2 — Traceability
Links and snapshots allow sources, relationships, and judgments to be inspected. The frozen acceptance criterion requires reconstruction by an evaluator; this observation is not yet available. Automated edge checks establish only that link targets exist. CL2 must not be reported as fully satisfied.

R-02 preserves `exclusion_warrant: null` and an empty list of contrary arguments. However, it does not generate a directed explanation of why the contrary chain is absent. Its conclusion is unchanged, but the explanation requires inspecting the implementation and snapshot. This is recorded as an explanatory discrepancy against the explicit-reasons requirement and as an issue for human review. The engine was not corrected after this observation.

## CL3 — Explicit Representation
- **Tension:** r002/r003 and O03 remain in A-01; no automatic compensation occurs.
- **Objection:** O-01 affects D02 and D04; O-02 preserves the objection and records its response. The program consumes a supplied acceptance judgment.
- **Constraint:** R-01 constructs a contrary chain through EXCLUDING-CONSTRAINT-1. R-02 does not conclude against justification without stipulated exclusionary force. Its incomplete explanation limits transparency.
- **Missing information:** M-01 retains E as an object whose information is absent, generates an informational issue, and limits D01/D02/D06. Absence is not treated as opposition.
- **Uncertainty:** U-01 retains alternatives with different support. U-02 retains uncertainty without blocking support when the supplied alternatives preserve the premise. This stability is limited to the declared set.

## CL5 — Reproducibility
The separate-process A-01 outputs match. This is effective, although limited, evidence of computational repetition, rather than merely a program capability to compare outputs. The same deterministic algorithm with identical inputs does not establish the adequacy of its grounds or agreement between researchers encoding a case.

## Relationship to the Contribution
The evidence shows how an instance can preserve relationships between evidence, context, candidate, and consequences; reconstruct supplied reasons or derive them from explicit links; and produce assessments with limitations. The contribution must be argued through the proposed relationships and conditions, supported by these applications. Agreement with design expectations does not establish external validity.
